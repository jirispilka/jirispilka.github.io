Jiří Spilka.

AI Engineer | AI Plumber | Practical problems meet data & AI 🤖💾

I work as an AI Engineer at Apify, helping to integrate the Apify platform into AI ecosystems.

I worked as a research and development engineer at Concerto.ai. There, I was part of a team developing conversational AI capable of handling question answering from both structured and unstructured data, scheduling appointments, and more.
I hold a Ph.D. in Artificial Intelligence and Biocybernetics from the Czech Technical University in Prague. My research there mainly focused on signal processing and machine learning, particularly in analyzing fetal heart rate. I also worked as a researcher at the Czech Institute of Informatics, Robotics, and Cybernetics. I had the pleasure of working with Patrice Abry during my post-doc at the Physics Department, ENS Lyon, France. Prior to that, I served as a Research and Development Engineer in the Cardiology Department at BTL Medical Technologies CZ, implementing signal processing and classification algorithms for Holter and resting ECGs.

I greatly enjoyed teaching an Artificial Intelligence course at the Department of Cybernetics, Faculty of Electrical Engineering, alongside Petr Posik and Radek Marik.
